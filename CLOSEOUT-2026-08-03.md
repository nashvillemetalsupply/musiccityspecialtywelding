## What shipped (all live, all verified in production)

### Custom CRM — "Lead Operations" at /ops

- **Persistence-first intake.** Every website quote is written to Neon Postgres
  (with photos to a private Vercel Blob store) *before* the notification email is
  attempted. A lead can no longer be lost to an email outage.
- **Attribution that makes money.** gclid + UTM + landing page + referrer are
  captured first-touch and stored on every lead. Won jobs with a gclid export as
  a Google Ads offline-conversion CSV (`Ads conversions` button) so Google can
  optimize toward jobs that actually paid, not just form fills.
- **The pipeline.** new → contacted → qualified → quoted → won/lost/spam with
  auto-timestamps, first-response tracking (speed-to-lead median on the board),
  estimate + revenue capture, interaction log (called/texted/emailed…),
  follow-up reminders (4h/1d/3d/1w quick picks), instant search, counted
  filters, pagination, CSV export, immutable event history per lead.
- **Manual entry.** Phone-in / walk-in / referral / repeat-customer leads join
  the same pipeline from a one-line form on the board.
- **Phone alerts (Web Push).** New leads and due follow-ups push straight to any
  phone that taps "Enable phone alerts" — independent of email, so an email
  failure alarm can't die with the email provider.
- **Automation.** Daily digest email (unanswered leads, due follow-ups, stale
  quotes, failed deliveries, conversions ready for Ads upload) + hourly
  follow-up reminder sweep + 5-minute external health monitor (GitHub Actions).
- **Security.** Passwordless magic-link sign-in (hashed one-time + session
  tokens), canonical-origin links, timing-safe cron auth, private photos behind
  an authenticated stream, /ops noindexed, CSV formula-injection guard.

## How the owner/workers sign in to the CRM

1. Go to **musiccityspecialtywelding.com/ops** on any phone or computer.
2. Enter the shop email: **sales@musiccityspecialtywelding.com**.
3. Open the "operations sign-in link" email in the shop inbox and tap it
   (works once, expires in 15 minutes; the device then stays signed in ~30 days).
4. Tap **Enable phone alerts** once per phone to get instant new-lead pushes.
5. Workers use the same shop mailbox. To move to a dedicated operator address
   later, set `OPS_LOGIN_EMAIL` in Vercel env.
6. Note: a signed-in session was left active in the shop Chrome profile from
   verification — that browser is ready to use as-is.

## QuickBooks and the CRM

QuickBooks keeps invoicing/payments; the CRM tracks the job's story. When a
QuickBooks payment lands: open the lead → enter **Final revenue** (marks won) →
tick **job completed** → paste the invoice number into Notes. The digest then
counts it toward the weekly "ready to upload to Google Ads" nudge. QuickBooks
sign-in itself is unchanged — no magic-link tie-in exists or is needed. A true
Intuit API sync is possible later but needs owner-approved Intuit credentials.
