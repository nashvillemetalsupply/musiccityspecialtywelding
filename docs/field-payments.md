# Field payments

Shop Brain hands in-person card collection to QuickBooks GoPayment and keeps QuickBooks as the money system of record. Card numbers, wallet credentials, and reader data never enter Shop Brain.

## One-time owner setup

1. Confirm the business has an approved QuickBooks Payments account.
2. In QuickBooks user management, invite each field employee with **Take payments only** or **Take payments only restricted** access. Do not share the owner login.
3. Install QuickBooks GoPayment on the employee's phone. On a supported iPhone, the owner or company admin can enable Tap to Pay; a Bluetooth QuickBooks Card Reader is the cross-platform alternative.
4. Process one owner-approved test invoice and confirm the QuickBooks receipt email reaches the connected shop Gmail account.

## On a job

1. Open the work order in Shop Brain and tap **Take payment**.
2. Open GoPayment, choose **Invoice payment**, and find the customer or the invoice number shown on the owner view.
3. Take the card, contactless wallet, cash, or check in GoPayment and send the customer receipt there.
4. Wait for the authenticated QuickBooks receipt. Shop Brain files it automatically only when its invoice number matches exactly one work order; an unmatched or ambiguous receipt waits in **Updates** for the owner to attach.
5. Once that receipt is filed, do not also use **Record what changed hands** for the same transaction. Entering it manually would count the same money twice.

Use **Record what changed hands** only for cash, checks, or payments taken outside QuickBooks. Payment remains separate from **Finish work** and **Close job**.
