The Lead Board
Owner's Guide

Every job that comes to the shop — from the first phone ring to the paid invoice — lives on one board. This guide covers everything: signing in, working leads, money, invoices, and the emails that go out under your name.

01Signing in — no passwords, ever

There is no sign-up. Only the shop's own mailbox can get in, so there's no password to forget or steal.

  1. On any phone or computer, go to musiccityspecialtywelding.com/ops
  2. Type the shop email: sales@musiccityspecialtywelding.com
  3. Open that inbox. An email called "operations sign-in link" arrives in seconds. Tap the button.
  4. You're in — and that device stays signed in about 30 days. Do it once per phone/computer.

Employees

Workers sign in the exact same way, using the same shop mailbox — have them do the link step while someone who can open the shop inbox forwards or taps it. Every action they take on a ticket is recorded in that ticket's history. If you later want a separate operator address, that's a one-line settings change — ask your web person.

Lost the link? Links work once and die after 15 minutes. Just request a fresh one — there's nothing to reset.

02Turn on phone alerts (do this first)

  1. Signed in on your phone, tap "Enable phone alerts" at the top of the board.
  2. Your phone asks permission for notifications — tap Allow.

From then on your phone buzzes the moment a lead hits the board — name, job, phone number; tapping it opens the ticket — and again when a follow-up reminder comes due. Works for every phone that turns it on (you and the crew).

If the button says "notifications blocked in browser settings", notifications are turned off for the whole browser on that device. On iPhone: add the site to the Home Screen first, then enable. On Android: Site settings → Notifications → Allow.

03Reading the board

  • The gauge — your median time from a lead arriving to the first call-back. Green under 15 minutes. This number is the single biggest thing that wins jobs.
  • NEEDS YOU NOW — the strip at the top: overdue reminders and leads nobody has called back. Each card has one-tap Call and Text.
  • The tiles — new leads, awaiting call-back, follow-ups due, won this month, all-time revenue, quotes on the street, failed email deliveries (should always be 0).
  • Job tickets — every lead is a paper ticket: number, name, job, where it came from (a Google ad, Google search, or direct), the rubber stamp (its status), the dollar value, and Call / Text / Open buttons.
  • Heat edges — a ticket nobody has called gets an orange edge after 1 hour, red after 4. Red edges cost money.
  • Search & filters — search any name, phone, job, or note. Filter chips show counts per stage.

04Working a lead, start to finish

  1. Phone buzzes / ticket appears. Tap Call on the ticket. Talk to them.
  2. Open the ticket → "Mark first response." That stops the clock on the gauge. If you already talked, use "Log a touch" (called / texted / left voicemail) — the first touch counts automatically.
  3. Set a reminder — in 4 hours, tomorrow, 3 days, next week. The board emails and buzzes you when it's due. Nothing gets forgotten.
  4. Save the estimate. Enter the dollar figure — the ticket is stamped QUOTED. If they gave an email, tick "email this estimate to the customer" and they get a clean branded quote.
  5. Win it: save Final revenue. Enter what it actually paid — stamped WON, counted in revenue. Tick "job completed" when the work's done, and optionally "send the thank-you email."
  6. Lost it or junk? Stamp it lost or spam — a reason is required, so six months from now you know why.

Phone and walk-in customers

Tap "+ Write up a phone or walk-in lead" on the board — name, phone, job, how it came in. Twenty seconds, and every job you get lives in one place, not just the website ones.

05Invoices — made in QuickBooks, chased by the board

You keep sending invoices exactly the way you do today, from QuickBooks. QuickBooks stays the money system of record. The board's job is making sure no invoice gets forgotten:

  1. Send the invoice from QuickBooks like always.
  2. Open the job's ticket → Money section → type the QuickBooks invoice #, pick the terms (due on receipt / net 7 / 14 / 30) → "Invoice is out."
  3. The ticket now shows INV #1337 out — and turns red OVERDUE past the due date.
  4. Every morning's digest email lists every unpaid invoice until the money lands.
  5. When QuickBooks shows the payment, enter it as Final revenue on the ticket. That marks it paid and closes the chase.
Why not send invoices from the board? Two systems both making invoices means two versions of your money. QuickBooks bills; the board remembers. One truth each.

07What runs by itself

  • Every website lead is saved to the shop's own database before any email is sent — a lead can never be lost to an email outage. If email ever fails, your phone push says so and the ticket is flagged.
  • Job photos are stored privately — only signed-in operators can see them.
  • An outside watchdog checks the site every 5 minutes, and the reminder sweep runs hourly.
  • Every lead remembers whether it came from a Google ad (down to the click), Google search, or direct. The "Ads conversions" button exports won jobs as a file you upload to Google Ads (Tools → Conversions → Uploads) so Google learns which clicks became paid work — that makes your ad money smarter over time.
  • Export CSV downloads every lead as a spreadsheet whenever you want your data.
